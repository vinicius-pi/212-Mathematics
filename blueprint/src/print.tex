\author{Vinícius Marques Pimenta}

\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
We present the formalization blueprint for \texttt{Mathematics212}, a Lean~4 (\texttt{v4.34.0}) and Rocq~\texttt{9.0} development covering three interconnected pillars: (i) Markov--Bernstein polynomial inequalities and discrete reciprocal-grid bounds; (ii) a measurable equioscillation Remez inequality driving an all-radii reciprocal Gevrey-1 asymptotic expansion with holomorphic coefficient profiles; and (iii) coprime M\"obius convolutions, local Euler-factor bounds, and Mellin multiplier identities and analytic obstructions. All 49 Lean submodules build without \texttt{sorry} or custom axioms, verified by \texttt{lake build}, \texttt{leanchecker}, and \texttt{rocqchk}.
\end{abstract}
